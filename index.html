How much does a website cost?
Straight numbers: $25 one-off for a complete one-page website under the October offer (normally £650), until 7 November. A premium Starter site is £325–£350, a full Business site is £1,990, and booking or e-commerce is £3,950. Optional ongoing care is £60/month — never required.
How long does it take?
One-page sites are usually live in days — often 3–5 working days. Bigger sites take 1–3 weeks, with a clear timeline agreed before we start.
What do I need to provide?
Just your business basics: what you do, your services or prices, and any photos or logo you already have. If something's missing, we guide you and use clean placeholders until you're ready.
What if I don't like the design?
You approve a free preview before paying anything, and revisions are generous — we adjust together until it feels right for your business.
How does payment work?
Simple and safe: you see and approve the preview first, then pay by PayPal or bank transfer. No surprise invoices — the quote you get is the price you pay.
Do I own my website?
Completely. Your site, your domain, your content. No lock-in, no hostage hosting — you can move or manage it yourself anytime.
What happens after launch?
Your site is yours to run. If you'd like us to handle updates and edits, the optional care plan is £60/month — cancel anytime. Managing it yourself is equally fine.
Can you fix my existing site?
Yes — slow, broken or phone-unfriendly sites are our specialty. A one-page rebuild is $25 under the October offer; bigger jobs are quoted after a free look.
